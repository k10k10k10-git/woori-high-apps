## Design Specificity Verdict

The site is highly specific to this ministry. Warm ministry copy, role-aware entry points, GBS, new-friend, retreat, and iGuard language, and current/next field work create a coherent product world. The weakness is prioritization rather than identity: the super-admin home and retreat administration often present the system taxonomy before the user's immediate decision.

The deterministic detector reported 50 raw warnings across 30 files. Forty-six `gray-on-color` warnings were false positives caused by conditional branches, hover states, sibling tokens, selector bleed, or low-opacity tint. Two `side-tab` findings are credible repeated decorative patterns on `.home-domain-card::before` and `.guide-card::before`; the active cue and unassigned-teacher warning borders are legitimate operational exceptions.

No reliable user-visible overlay is available. Mutable browser injection failed because the evaluation surface exposed a read-only document title. The signed-in production DOM and native screenshot were used as fallback visual evidence.

## Overall Impression

A credible operational product with unusually strong domain modeling and responsive adaptation. The mobile field cue card is calm, task-specific, and reassuring. Mobile home and retreat administration are weakest because navigation, status, and actions compete.

## What's Working

1. **Strong product-workflow fit.** Field UI prioritizes staff identity, location, current work, next work, and timeline instead of generic dashboard metrics.
2. **Responsive information architecture.** Semester and field navigation are regrouped for mobile rather than merely scaled down, and sensitive contact columns are omitted from the mobile DOM.
3. **Strong reassurance states.** Skeletons, active states, read-only banners, offline guidance, and current/next task cards are consistently present.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] High-impact roster operations lack a preview and confirmation boundary

- **Why it matters:** Administrators cannot confidently understand how many records or fields will change, and the restore path is not visible enough.
- **Fix:** Show changed, preserved, and conflicted counts and fields before a separate apply step, with a visible backup and restore path.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] Retreat administration exposes too much taxonomy at once

- **Why it matters:** Users interpret five domain tabs, a second tab row, filters, metrics, synchronization tools, exceptions, and roster content before addressing the current task.
- **Fix:** Preserve routes and tabs, but lead with `검토 필요 → 명단 준비 → 편성 → 현장 준비` state summaries and progressively disclose rare tools.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] Mobile home buries the role-specific next action

- **Why it matters:** A user with 30 seconds must scroll past greeting and domain architecture before reaching the work queue.
- **Fix:** Put the next schedule and role-specific primary action in the first mobile viewport, moving domain introduction cards later.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Home schedule modal diverges from the shared overlay contract

- **Why it matters:** Keyboard focus can escape behind the dialog and Escape behavior depends on focus placement.
- **Fix:** Move it to `OverlayShell` with focus trap, explicit close, Escape, and focus restoration.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable audit`

### [P2] Mobile field selection is not preserved after refresh

- **Why it matters:** Interruption or refresh returns users to the cue card and the top-heavy navigation is inefficient for one-handed use.
- **Fix:** Synchronize the selected view into the URL and evaluate a reachable, accessible field navigation pattern.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`
